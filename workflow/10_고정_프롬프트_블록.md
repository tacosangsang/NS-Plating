# 10. 고정 프롬프트 블록 (원문)

`[예외: 이 문서 전체는 PLATING 스타일·설정]` 다른 프로젝트에서는 **블록을 나누는 방식과 바뀐 이유**만 가져간다. STYLE·AVOID는 원문 그대로 붙이고 고치지 않는다는 운영 원칙도 일반.

## 1. 구조

이미지: 공통 베이스(STYLE + WORLD & TONE) → 씬별 [SCENE] 모듈 → 공통 AVOID → (해당 시) 전용 금지 줄. 카메라는 모듈에 넣지 않고 컷표에서 가져와 모듈 뒤에 한 줄. 공통 블록만 약 1179자, 최상층은 전용 금지까지 약 1521자 → 모듈 + 컷 문장은 약 1479자 안에(3000자 제한).

영상: REFERENCE ROLES → STYLE → SUMMARY → SPACE AND LIGHT → TIMELINE → (사람) FACE RULE / EYES / 손 / 동일 디자인 / OUTFIT / STAGING → CONSISTENCY → AVOID (05 문서).

## 2. STYLE (10-31판, 사용자 지시)

```
STYLE: feature-length 3D animated film CG render, smooth fluid motion, clean polished CG surfaces with real material detail (cloth weave, rusted metal, worn wood); color grading inspired by LAIKA films: muted earthy desaturated palette, deep rich shadows, soft warm-cool contrast; naturalistic cinematic lighting; stylised character design with real adult human proportions and real-world scale (an adult is about 1.8 m tall); wide and medium shots in deep focus with natural aerial perspective, shallow focus only in close-ups.
```

10-28판에서 바꾼 이유(10-30 미니어처 분석):
- "shallow depth of field, soft volumetric haze"는 틸트시프트처럼 작아 보이게 → 와이드·미디엄은 deep focus + 공기 원근, 얕은 심도는 클로즈업에만.
- "not their stop-motion technique"는 부정문이어도 스톱모션 쪽으로 끌어당김 → 단어를 아예 빼고 "feature-length 3D animated film CG render".
- "slightly stylised doll proportions"는 인형 느낌과 통통한 손의 원인 → 실제 성인 비율과 축척(1.8m).


스타일 이력: 10-27 이전은 스톱모션·클레이·수제 질감 → 10-28 사용자가 "3D 애니메이션 + 라이카 색감만"으로 변경 → 10-31 부정문까지 삭제. 10-28 이전 시트는 스톱모션 질감.

## 3. WORLD & TONE (이미지 공통)

```
[WORLD & TONE] a dystopian fable where insects are the only food allowed to the poor; a vertical, class-divided city staged as a recurring contrast between a warm, grimy, lived-in underclass and an elite summit that is artificial, ritualistic and arranged as precisely as a plated dish; nature at the top is fake and curated; food plated as art, people arranged like food; uncanny yet grounded, quiet unease, socially critical.
```

## 4. AVOID (공통, 10-31)

같은 이유로 "miniature diorama, stop-motion, claymation, puppet" 단어를 뺐다.
```
AVOID: realistic photo humans, glossy plastic skin, toy-like scale, tilt-shift look, readable text, letters, logos, extra limbs, hexagon, honeycomb, mesh or diamond tile patterns on fabric, choppy frame-by-frame animation.
```
클립·컷 전용 금지는 다음 줄에 "No ~." 문장으로.

**최상층 전용 금지 (AVOID 다음 줄)**
```
No chandelier, no marble floor, no chrome, no red carpet, no skyscraper penthouse, no restaurant interior, no straight banquet table, no one-point perspective corridor table, no industrial power plant, no steam plume, no smoke, no tall bell-shaped birdcage, no onion dome, no tall spire finial, no hemispherical cage, no food cloche, no owl.
```

## 5. 사람 블록 (하층민·요리사·도둑·공물. 진우·흰토끼·상류층 제외)

**FACE RULE + EYES + 손**
```
FACE RULE: The face is not a shadow. It is a flat matte black surface, part of the character design, like black velvet. It stays pure black under any lighting, even when light shines straight into the hood. The face surface has no skin, no nose, no mouth, no eyebrows, no eyelids.
EYES: two small, close-set, round eyes, the only features on the face. Each eye is a thin white ring around a large glossy black pupil that fills the ring, with one tiny white catchlight dot at the upper right of the pupil. The pupils are glossy, the face around them is matte.
The hands are peach skin.
```
옛 문구 "no facial features" + "white ring eyes"만 쓰면 눈동자가 지워져 흰 고리만 남는다. 반드시 EYES 문장.

**EYES (CHILD)** — 아이 컷에서 EYES를 이것으로
```
EYES (CHILD): two big round eyes, clearly larger than an adult's, the only features on the face. Each eye has a wide soft white eyeball around a large round dark brown-black iris in the centre, with one tiny white catchlight dot at the upper right of the iris. The eyes are glossy, the face around them is matte.
```

**얼굴 반사가 생길 때 추가**
```
No sweat and no reflection on the black face surface; the eye catchlights stay.
```

**동일 디자인 (군중 둘 이상)**
```
All of them are identical in design and cannot be told apart as individuals; only patch positions, height and pose differ.
```

**STAGING (여러 명)**
```
STAGING: The people stand scattered irregularly, not in rows or lines and not directly behind one another. Place them along loose diagonals at uneven distances, some closer to the camera and some farther back, overlapping only partly, each turned at a slightly different angle. No grid, no queue, no symmetrical arrangement, no evenly spaced figures.
```
빼는 경우: 연회장 착석, 공물·피난 행렬처럼 줄을 세우는 컷(배치를 직접 적는다). 눕거나 앉은 장면은 "The people here are lying, sitting or leaning, not standing" 추가.

**OUTFIT (군중 판초)**
```
OUTFIT: pointed tan burlap hood with a soft peak framing the black face, burlap poncho tied at the neck with a twisted rope cord knotted at the front, jagged frayed hem, loose knee-length burlap shorts, cream cloth strips wrapped around the shins, bare peach-skin feet. Square stitched patches with cross-stitched corners in ONLY muted blue-gray, dark umber brown and brick red. ABSOLUTELY NO green, olive or khaki-green. No jewelry, no bracelet, no props except the elder's walking stick.
```

**OUTFIT (공물, 냅킨 옷)** — 손이 안 보이므로 손 문장은 빼도 됨
```
OUTFIT: wrapped in one deep wine-red dinner napkin; one corner folded up over the head into a stiff pointed hood framing the black face; both arms wrapped inside the napkin, pressed against the body, no hands visible; the napkin narrows toward the knees and ends in one pointed corner at mid-calf; bare lower legs and bare feet. No belt, no buttons, no ties, no jewelry, no patches, no pattern.
```

**BASE OUTFIT (요리사·도둑 공통, 행주 옷)** — 목 천이 얼굴 아랫부분을 덮는다
```
BASE OUTFIT: warm oatmeal beige linen like a kitchen hand towel, fine plain weave. Close-fitting pointed hood with one slate-grey stripe running diagonally across it. A strip of the same linen wound in a few layers around the neck, rising to the lower edge of the hood opening and covering only the bottom of the black face, well below the eyes. Long-sleeved wrap tunic, front panels overlapping in a crossover V (wearer's left over right), one slate-grey stripe along the overlapping edge, diagonal hem just above the knee. Forearms wound with narrow linen strips like bandages, ending at a slate-grey cuff band. Straight loose oatmeal trousers gathered at the ankle. Plain black rubber kitchen clogs.
```

**COOK APRON (보존파 요리사, 베이스 뒤)**
```
COOK APRON: clean matte charcoal grey cotton twill, crisp and new, no stains. Straight bib apron with a square top, one horizontal welt chest pocket, straight hem just below the knee. Thin charcoal webbing neck straps with small antique bronze slider buckles; on the back the straps cross in an X through one small round antique bronze ring and clip to the apron's back corners at waist height with small bronze clips. The skirt wraps just past the side seams; the oatmeal tunic shows between the edges at the back. No lower pocket, no ties, nothing hanging.
```

**THIEF APRON (도둑, 훔친 진우 앞치마)**
```
THIEF APRON: Jinwoo's olive green canvas bib apron, shin length, darker olive-brown stains, faded edges, raw frayed hem, small bib pocket with a narrow pen slot, wide lower pocket with one divider, a ragged hole near the hem on the wearer's right, copper rivets. Dark brown leather neck strap with a dull brass buckle on the wearer's left; on the back two leather straps cross in an X through one round brass ring. The waist cords are cut: only a 3 cm frayed olive cord stub sticks out of each waist eyelet, no knot, nothing hangs, the sides hang loose at the back.
```

**LAYOUT INSIDE THE TRUCK (HOPE 실내 컷)** — 실내 시트가 밖에서 본 그림이라 안쪽 카메라에서 좌우가 뒤집히거나 설비가 빠진다 → 화면 기준 배치를 글로 고정.
```
LAYOUT INSIDE THE TRUCK: the serving window is cut into one long side wall, the alley side, with a long metal counter running along it under the window. The fixed fittings stand along the opposite long wall, the sea side, facing the window: from the cab end to the rear end, first the stove, then the sink, then the low cabinet with a worn wooden top and the jars on it; the wooden shelf with one small metal box hangs above the cabinet. The sink and the stove are fixed in place and are always there. The rear end wall holds the rear door with one small window; an empty hook is on that wall just right of the rear door as seen from the cab end. The side door, with its own small window, is on the window wall between the serving window and the rear corner. The cab end has a narrow door to the cab.
```
운전석 쪽에서 뒷문을 보면(12번 구도) 판매창이 왼쪽, 설비 벽이 오른쪽. 뒤 끝에서 운전석을 보면(11번) 반대. 컷마다 화면 기준으로 한 번 더.

### 적용 규칙
- 사람이 안 나오거나 얼굴이 안 보일 만큼 먼 컷은 FACE RULE·EYES 생략(넣으면 카메라가 다가간다). 판초 실루엣과 동일 디자인만.
- 입 가림 금지(`No face wraps or mouth covers on the people.`)는 목 천 없는 군중 컷에만. 요리사·도둑은 목 천이 얼굴 아랫부분을 덮는다.
- 진우는 FACE RULE 없음(정상 얼굴). 올리브색은 진우 앞치마·도둑 앞치마·진우 팔찌에만.
- 흰토끼는 하층민 규칙 해당 없음. 상류층은 조류 반가면, 입을 덮지 않는다. 흰 토끼에 ② HUMAN+MASK와 가면용 AVOID를 적용하지 않는다.
